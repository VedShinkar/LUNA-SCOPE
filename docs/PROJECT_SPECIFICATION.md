# LUNA-SCOPE — Product Specification

## Vision
Provide an integrated, scientifically traceable environment for evaluating lunar landing sites, landing dates, and mission equipment configurations.

## Target users
- Lunar mission planners and aerospace engineers
- Planetary scientists and researchers
- Rover/lander development teams
- Educators, students, and the public

## Core capabilities
1. Lunar Intelligence Explorer
2. Celestial Geometry Engine
3. Solar Power & Illumination Intelligence
4. Direct-to-Earth Communication Analyzer
5. Lunar Mission Time Machine
6. Mission Decision Intelligence
7. Landing Hazard & Terrain Safety Analyzer
8. Thermal Environment & Survival Predictor
9. Rover Traverse & Mobility Planner
10. Permanently Shadowed Region & Resource Intelligence
11. Mission Operations & Activity Window Planner
12. Lunar Dust & Surface Environment Risk
13. Navigation, Positioning & Coverage Intelligence
14. Mission Contingency Simulator
15. Scientific Data Confidence & Uncertainty Analyzer
16. Lunar Infrastructure Placement Optimizer
17. Lunar Mission Digital Twin

## Cross-cutting capabilities
- AI Mission Copilot (must use verified scientific service outputs)
- Integrated simulation environment
- Configurable lander, rover, and surface-infrastructure profiles
- Data provenance, uncertainty, and validation

## First complete workflow
Select site and date -> load terrain -> calculate Sun/Earth geometry -> evaluate illumination and visibility -> configure equipment -> compare scenarios -> produce traceable assessment.

## Out of scope for the initial release
- Spacecraft command and control
- Certified landing guidance
- Claims of guaranteed landing safety
- Unvalidated real-time dust/weather prediction
- Unsupported claims of confirmed water ice
