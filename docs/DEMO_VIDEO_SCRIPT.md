# LUNA-SCOPE — 30-second NASA Space Apps demo video script

**Target length:** 27–30 seconds. Speak naturally; keep the screen recording moving. Replace the bracketed team-name and member-name details before recording.

**0:00–0:04 | Introduction**
"Hi, we're [TEAM NAME], and we built LUNA-SCOPE for the lunar landing-site navigator challenge."

**0:04–0:09 | The problem**
"Choosing a lunar landing site isn't just about the surface. You also need to understand sunlight, terrain and communication with Earth."

**0:09–0:22 | Show the product**
"LUNA-SCOPE brings those factors into one place. We use NASA lunar terrain data, explore Sun and Earth visibility over time, compare candidate regions, and use a mission assistant to navigate the results."

**0:22–0:30 | Why it matters**
"It gives mission planners a clearer way to explore trade-offs and uncertainties before making decisions. That's LUNA-SCOPE."

## Recording plan

- 0:00–0:04: Team title card and project name.
- 0:04–0:09: Lunar south-pole visual and the three factors: terrain, sunlight, Earth link.
- 0:09–0:16: Screen recording of terrain explorer and site selection.
- 0:16–0:22: Operations Timeline and comparison view; briefly show the Copilot.
- 0:22–0:30: End card with tagline: **Explore the terrain. Understand the windows. Compare the mission.**

## Accuracy notes for the narration and screen capture

- Say "NASA lunar terrain data" only for the actual LOLA products used in the build.
- Do not call the Copilot a live LLM. It currently uses deterministic question routing to project tools.
- Do not present synthetic Mission Intelligence fixture scores as NASA-derived rankings or landing probabilities. If a synthetic example appears on screen, show its synthetic/demo label.
- Avoid saying the system certifies landing safety. It is an exploration and decision-support prototype.
- If any generated images or video are used, include the visible SI-generated watermark required by the NASA Space Apps submission guide.
- Keep the video at or below 30 seconds. The NASA guide also allows a slide demo of up to seven slides, including title and introduction slides.
